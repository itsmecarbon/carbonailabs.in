Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

  • Plans are monthly, and you can cancel at any time.
  • Cancelling stops the next charge. Your plan stays on until the end of the month you paid for.
  • We refund in full any duplicate or wrong charge, and any charge taken after you cancelled.

This policy applies to the monthly plans that coaches and gyms buy for Coach AI and CarbonFit AI. Carbon AI Labs is a sole proprietorship owned by Ajay Andrews, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Contact us at ajayandrews@carbonailabs.in or +91 97907 49165.

1. How to cancel

  • In the coach area of the app, open Plan and billing and cancel your subscription. You can also email ajayandrews@carbonailabs.in from your registered email address.
  • Cancelling takes effect at the end of your current paid month. Until then you keep full access, and you will not be charged again.
  • There is no cancellation fee and no lock-in period.

2. Refunds

SituationRefund

3. How refunds are paid

  • To ask for a refund, email ajayandrews@carbonailabs.in with your registered email and the Razorpay payment ID or date.
  • We reply within 2 working days. Once a refund is approved, we start it within 5 working days.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. Your bank or UPI app usually shows the refund within 5 to 7 working days after we start it.

4. Clients of coaches

If you are a client paying your coach or gym for coaching, your payment and any refund are between you and your coach or gym. Please contact them directly.
