Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

  • We collect only what the apps need to make training and meal plans and to run your account.
  • Client data in Coach AI and CarbonFit AI is stored in India (Mumbai) and is seen by the client, their coach or gym, and by us only when we need to keep the service running.
  • We never sell personal data and never use it for advertising.
  • You can see, correct, download or delete your data at any time.

This policy explains how Carbon AI Labs handles personal data across our website (carbonailabs.in) and our apps, Coach AI and CarbonFit AI. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. Carbon AI Labs is a sole proprietorship owned by Ajay Andrews, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Contact us at ajayandrews@carbonailabs.in or +91 97907 49165.

2. What we collect

Clients and members

  • Profile: first name, age, sex, height, weight, goal, training experience and diet preferences.
  • Health information you choose to give: health conditions, injuries, medication and pain. This is sensitive, and the app always sends it to your coach in person first. No automatic plan is made from it.
  • Progress: weekly check-ins, body measurements and, only if your coach uses photo check-ins, the progress photos you choose to upload.
  • Messages between you and your coach.
  • Sign-in: your mobile number is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, along with a hashed PIN. We cannot read either one back.
  • Coaching package details and their end date.

Coaches and gyms

  • Name, business name, email, phone number, chosen plan, brand name and logo (if you upload one) and app settings.
  • Billing records: plan, payment status and the payment and subscription IDs from Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details; Razorpay handles those.

Website visitors

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser shares your IP address with Google when the page loads.
  • If you email or call us, we keep your message and contact details so we can reply.

3. Why we use it

  • To make, adjust and send workout and meal plans, and to let clients and coaches message each other.
  • To run accounts, sign-in, security and support.
  • To bill coaches and gyms for their plan.
  • To meet legal duties, such as keeping tax and payment records.

Clients give consent when they sign up, and the app shows the consent text first. Coaches' data is used to provide the service they subscribed to. We do not use personal data to train AI models, for advertising, or for any purpose not listed here.

4. Who can see it

  • The client, and their coach or gym team.
  • Carbon AI Labs, only to set up, support, secure or fix the service.
  • Service providers we use to run the apps:
    • Supabase stores the database and photos, in its Mumbai, India region.
    • Streamlit Community Cloud runs the app. It is hosted in the United States, so data passes through its servers while you use the app, but it is not stored there.
    • Razorpay handles payments.
    • Microsoft 365 handles our email.
    • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Authorities, only when Indian law requires it.

We never sell or rent personal data.

5. How long we keep it

  • Progress photos are deleted 12 months after they were taken.
  • Other client data is kept while the client works with the coach. It is deleted when the client deletes their account, withdraws consent, or the coach removes them.
  • When a coach or gym stops using our service, we delete their clients' data within 30 days of the end of the last paid period. The coach can download it before then.
  • Billing and tax records are kept for as long as tax law requires, usually 8 years.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • See the personal data we hold about you, and who it has been shared with.
  • Correct or update it.
  • Delete it, and withdraw consent at any time. Clients can do this in the app under My account, which also lets you download all your data.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you die or are unable to act.
  • Complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our reply.

Email ajayandrews@carbonailabs.in with the subject "Privacy request". We reply within 30 days at the latest.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database and photo storage are private and need sign-in. Phone numbers and PINs are hashed. Photos are shrunk, and location details are removed when they are uploaded. If a personal data breach happens, we will tell the people affected and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

8. Age

Our apps are meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 without verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete their data.
