About

A lab for real business problems

Carbon AI Labs was founded in Chennai in October 2026 by Ajay Andrews to research and develop solutions to business problems with artificial intelligence.

Most businesses lose hours to work that is slow, repetitive and easy to get wrong. We study how that work is done today, then build AI systems that do it with more speed and accuracy, so owners can make better decisions and earn more from the time they have.

Our first products serve gyms and independent coaches across India. If your business has a problem that eats your team's time, we would like to hear about it.

Problem first
We start from where a business loses time and money, not from the technology.
People decide
Sensitive calls go to a person. In our fitness apps, any injury, medication, pain or age flag goes to a coach.
Rules you can check
Our systems show their reasoning, and every rule names the source it comes from.
Measured in results
We judge our work by time saved, errors avoided and revenue gained.
